Data Services

Mission-driven organizations are awash in data: surveys, assessments, student information systems, CRMs, public datasets, and spreadsheets that each report a little differently. Teams chase metrics for funders, reconcile numbers across sources, and burn out analysts on a hamster wheel of QC and caveats.

We help organizations lead from their data instead of wrestling with it. We align sources, automate the routine work, and put clear, actionable findings in the hands of the people closest to the work, so improvement can happen at every level.

We usually build in what your team already uses, from Google Sheets and Apps Script to PostgreSQL, Airtable, and your survey platform, so the system keeps working after we leave.

Typical work includes

  • Dashboards and reporting, with permissions scoped from the network office to the school or site
  • Data pipelines and automation: scheduled feeds from vendors, SFTP drops, CRMs, and forms
  • Data quality and QC: validation, reconciliation, fuzzy matching, and de-identification
  • Analysis and insight that joins public data with your own
  • Survey design and measurement: instrument design, construct validation, norm-referenced reporting
  • Responsible AI and automation inside your environment, so your data never leaves it

How this connects

Data work rarely ends at the dashboard. The same findings usually need to reach a board, a funder, or a classroom, and the systems that hold the data are often the platforms we help teams select.
