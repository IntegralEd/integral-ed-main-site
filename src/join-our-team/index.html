Join Our Team

We are a 21-person studio of instructional designers, eLearning developers, media producers, and strategists doing work we love with organizations we value. As our partners grow, so do we, and we are always glad to meet designers and developers who share our standards and our values.

How we work together

Each engagement is led by a Project Director who serves as the single point of contact for the client and brings in the right expertise at the right time. Teammates collaborate across instructional design, eLearning development, media production, platforms, and strategy, with room to grow into new crafts as projects call for them.

Many of our teammates started as collaborators on a single project. If you are an instructional designer, Storyline or Rise developer, video producer, or learning strategist interested in working with us, we would love to hear from you.
